How I can help

Professional accountancy services designed to help individuals, sole traders and small businesses stay organised, compliant and confident.

  • Bookkeeping

    Recording your income and expenses, reconciling your bank accounts and keeping your records ready for tax time.

    Good for: sole traders and small businesses who want their records off their plate.

  • VAT returns

    Preparing and submitting your VAT returns to HMRC accurately and on time.

    Good for: VAT-registered businesses of any structure.

  • Personal tax

    Completing your Self Assessment tax return and explaining what you owe and when.

    Good for: sole traders, landlords, company directors and anyone who needs to file a tax return.

  • Year-end accounts

    Preparing your annual accounts and filing them with Companies House and HMRC.

    Good for: limited companies and businesses that need formal annual accounts.

  • Corporation tax returns

    Working out your company's corporation tax and filing the return with HMRC.

    Good for: limited companies.

  • Management accounts

    Regular management reports to help you monitor and improve your business performance.

    Good for: growing businesses that want a clear, regular picture of how they are doing.

  • Start-up support

    Helping new businesses build strong financial foundations from day one.

    Good for: new sole traders and limited companies getting set up.

  • Business support

    Ongoing financial advice and practical support to help your business grow with confidence.

    Good for: owners who want a trusted accountant to turn to throughout the year.

  • Financial health check

    Reviewing your finances and identifying practical ways to improve your business performance.

    Good for: any business that wants a fresh look at its numbers.

Common questions

When can I become a client?

Straight away. I'm taking on new clients now – send an enquiry and I'll get back to you within 2 working days.

Do you work with Deaf clients?

Yes. My practice welcomes Deaf, Deafblind, hard-of-hearing, visually impaired and hearing clients. Let me know in your enquiry how you'd like to communicate.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. Enquiries and a 20-minute introductory chat are free. Longer, in-depth advice sessions are charged, and the fee is taken off your first bill if you become a client.

How much do you charge?

You'll find starting prices and monthly packages in the Pricing section above. Once I've reviewed what you need, I'll send you a personalised quote, so you know the exact cost before any work starts.

What do I need to send you to get started?

I'll send you a simple checklist. Usually it's photo ID and proof of address, your Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR), and your recent records, such as bank statements, receipts and invoices.

Why do you need to check my identity?

Anti-money laundering law requires every accountant to check the identity of new clients before starting work. It's quick, and your documents are kept securely.

Do you work with clients outside London?

Yes. I'm based in London and work with clients across the UK, by email, text message and video call.

How do video calls work?

Meetings are on weekday evenings and Saturdays, by arrangement, and we agree the format in advance. Calls can include a BSL interpreter, live captions or the chat box – whatever works best for both of us. If you'd prefer, we can do everything by email instead.

Can you help if I'm behind with my tax or bookkeeping?

Yes. Many people come to an accountant when things have piled up. Tell me where you're at in your enquiry, with no judgement, and I'll suggest a plan to get you back on track.

Can I book an appointment directly?

Please send an enquiry first. Once I've reviewed it, I'll offer you an appointment if one is needed.

Ready to get started?

Whether you need help with bookkeeping, tax returns or ongoing business support, I'd be happy to discuss how I can help.
