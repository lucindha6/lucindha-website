What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website can save in your browser. They're often used to remember settings, count visitors or show adverts.

Cookies on this website

This website doesn't set any cookies. There's no analytics, advertising or social media tracking, and nothing is saved in your browser's storage.

The enquiry form sends your answers straight to me through my website hosting provider. It doesn't place a cookie on your device.

Other services the website uses

The fonts come from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google to download them, which shares your IP address with Google. Google Fonts doesn't set cookies. The privacy notice explains more.

Links to other websites, such as news stories, take you to sites with their own cookie policies.

If this changes

If I ever add cookies that aren't strictly necessary, such as visitor statistics, I'll ask for your permission first and update this page.

Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser's settings. This website will keep working normally.
