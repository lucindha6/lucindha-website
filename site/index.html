Accessible by design

Communication that works for you

My practice welcomes Deaf, Deafblind, hard-of-hearing, visually impaired and hearing clients. Tell me how you prefer to communicate and I'll adapt to suit you.

Tell me your communication preferences
  • Your choice of contact method – email, text, video call or face to face.
  • Plain-English explanations of your accounts and tax, without the jargon.
  • Captions, transcripts and BSL on video content as it's added.
  • A website built for everyone – high contrast, screen-reader and keyboard friendly.

About

My story

I'm an AAT Licensed Accountant and MAAT member, currently studying towards the Chartered Public Finance Accountant (CPFA) qualification through CIPFA. Alongside accounting, I also deliver coaching, public speaking and specialist workshops, combining professional expertise with real-world experience.

As a profoundly Deaf British Sign Language (BSL) user, I understand first-hand how communication barriers affect access to professional services, and I'm committed to making my services accessible for both Deaf and hearing clients. BSL interpreters can be arranged where required.
