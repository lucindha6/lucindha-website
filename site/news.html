  2. 21 March 2026Her Football Hub· Match report

    England crowned European Deaf Futsal champions

    England's deaf women went unbeaten in Croatia to win the European Deaf Futsal Championship. Lucindha scored twice in the opening win over Poland and both goals in the final against them.

  3. 8 January 2026Tamil Guardian· Interview

    Bronze at the Tokyo Deaflympics

    Lucindha talks about winning bronze with Great Britain's deaf women's football team in Tokyo, raising around £4,000 herself to get there, and why she campaigns for government funding and better deaf education.

  4. 9 November 2025Thamarai· Feature

    Representing England at the Deaflympics in Tokyo

    A profile of Lucindha as a British Tamil deaf footballer heading to her fourth Deaflympics. It covers losing her brother in 2016, a diagnosis of a rare inherited heart condition, and how Deaflympians must fund their own places on the team.
