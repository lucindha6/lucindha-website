  1. 23 March 2026England FootballMatch report

    Two goals in the European final

    England's deaf women were crowned European futsal champions in Croatia with a 2-0 win over Poland in the final. Lucindha scored both goals, and captain Zara Musker said she took her chances brilliantly.

    Read on England Football (opens in a new tab)

  2. 21 March 2026Her Football HubMatch report

    England crowned European Deaf Futsal champions

    England's deaf women went unbeaten in Croatia to win the European Deaf Futsal Championship. Lucindha scored twice in the opening win over Poland and both goals in the final against them.

    Read on Her Football Hub (opens in a new tab)

  3. 8 March 2026England FootballSquad profiles

    Meet the England women's deaf squad

    Before the European Championship, captain Zara Musker introduced the squad. She named Lucindha as one of the most improved players, said the team relies on her experience to get into scoring areas, and described her as the one who keeps the mood light.

    Read on England Football (opens in a new tab)

  4. 20 February 2026England FootballSquad announcement

    Selected for the 2026 European Deaf Futsal Championship

    Lucindha was named in England's 13-player women's deaf squad for the European Championship finals in Poreč, Croatia, in March 2026.

    Read on England Football (opens in a new tab)

  5. 8 January 2026Tamil GuardianInterview

    Bronze at the Tokyo Deaflympics

    Lucindha talks about winning bronze with Great Britain's deaf women's football team in Tokyo, raising around £4,000 herself to get there, and why she campaigns for government funding and better deaf education.

    Read on Tamil Guardian (opens in a new tab)

  6. 9 November 2025ThamaraiFeature

    Representing England at the Deaflympics in Tokyo

    A profile of Lucindha as a British Tamil deaf footballer heading to her fourth Deaflympics. It covers losing her brother in 2016, a diagnosis of a rare inherited heart condition, and how Deaflympians must fund their own places on the team.

    Read on Thamarai (opens in a new tab)
