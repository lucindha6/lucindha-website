Professional body and licence

Lucindha Lawson is licensed and regulated by AAT under licence number 1010532.

Lucindha Lawson is a full member (MAAT) of the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) and an AAT licensed accountant, licence no. 1010532.

The practice follows AAT's standards and Code of Professional Ethics (opens in a new tab), including its rules on professional conduct, client care and continuing professional development.

Anti-money laundering supervision

The practice is supervised by AAT for anti-money laundering purposes under the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017.

This means I must check the identity of every new client before starting work, keep those records and report any suspicion of money laundering to the authorities.

Professional indemnity insurance

The practice holds professional indemnity insurance, as AAT requires. The insurer's name and contact details and the territorial coverage of the policy are available on request.

Data protection

The company is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). The privacy notice explains how personal information is handled.

Engagement and complaints

Every accountancy client receives a written engagement letter before work begins, setting out the scope of the work, fees and terms of business. If something goes wrong, the complaints procedure explains what to do, including how to escalate to AAT.
