Community Work

Public Speaking

Inspiring audiences through lived experience, leadership and inclusion.

Whether speaking to schools, businesses, charities, conferences or sports organisations, I enjoy sharing my journey to inspire confidence, resilience and a greater understanding of Deaf culture and inclusion.

From experiencing language deprivation as a child and living with Long QT syndrome to competing as an international Deaf athlete and building a professional career in accountancy, my journey has been shaped by resilience, determination and the belief that with the right support and opportunities, barriers can be overcome.

Speaking Topics

Every audience is different. Whether I’m speaking to schools, businesses, charities or sporting organisations, every talk is tailored to your audience, objectives and event.

  • Deaf Awareness

    Helping organisations better understand Deaf culture, communication and inclusion.

  • Women’s Sport

    Sharing experiences of competing as an elite female Deaf athlete.

  • Disability, Diversity & Inclusion (EDI)

    Sharing practical insights into Deaf culture, accessibility and inclusive communication, helping organisations create environments where everyone can thrive.

  • Resilience

    Sharing how I’ve overcome challenges, including language deprivation, Long QT syndrome and the pressures of elite sport, while maintaining a positive mindset and determination to succeed.

  • Inclusion & Accessibility

    Creating environments where everyone feels valued and included.

  • Education, Language & Career

    From experiencing language deprivation as a child to becoming a graduate accountant and international athlete, I share how education, determination and the right support can transform lives.

  • Role Model

    Encouraging young people to believe in themselves and achieve their ambitions.

  • Elite Sport

    What it takes to compete at the highest international level, perform under pressure and develop a high-performance mindset.

  • Question & Answer Sessions

    Interactive question and answer sessions, encouraging open conversations tailored to your audience.
