Community Work

Sport & Coaching

Helping athletes, teams and organisations develop on and off the court through coaching, leadership and lived experience.

Alongside my finance career, I represent England internationally in Deaf football and futsal and coach players of different ages and abilities. I enjoy helping individuals and teams reach their potential through practical coaching, mentoring and education.

Contact Lucindha

How I’m Involved in Sport

Sport has shaped who I am. Through representing England, competing at the highest level of Deaf sport and volunteering in youth football, I aim to inspire the next generation and make the game more inclusive for everyone.

  • Back-to-Back European Champion

    Proud member of the England Deaf Women’s Futsal team, winning the European Championship in 2022 and 2026.

  • Four-time Deaflympian

    Represented Great Britain at four Deaflympic Games (2013, 2017, 2023 and 2025), competing at the highest level of Deaf football and futsal.

  • England Deaf Futsal International

    Representing England on the international stage, competing against the world’s best Deaf futsal teams.

  • International Experience

    Competing internationally has developed resilience, leadership, teamwork and the ability to perform under pressure. These qualities shape everything I do both on and off the court.

  • Heathlands School

    Volunteering at Heathlands School to inspire Deaf young people through football, futsal and positive role modelling.

  • St Albans City DFC Youth

    Supporting the development of young players through volunteer coaching.

  • FA Para Talent Hubs

    Supporting talented young players through the FA Para Football Talent Hubs at Bloomsbury and Watford.

  • Role Model

    Sharing my journey to encourage girls and young women to believe in themselves and pursue their ambitions.

  • Giving Back

    Passionate about making football and futsal more inclusive, supporting the next generation and creating opportunities within Deaf sport.

Why Invite Lucindha?

From international competition to grassroots football, I use my experiences to inspire others, promote inclusion and encourage the next generation to achieve their full potential.

  • Elite Experience

    Representing England and Great Britain at the highest international level of Deaf football and futsal.

  • Inspiring Others

    Encouraging young people to build confidence, resilience and ambition through sport.

  • Community Impact

    Supporting schools, clubs and FA Para Football Talent Hubs through volunteering, mentoring, advocacy and community engagement.

  • Inclusive Leadership

    Championing Deaf awareness, accessibility and equal opportunities through sport.
